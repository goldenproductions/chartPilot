# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.

## The longer version

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

Examples of behaviour that make this a good place to work:

- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Being explicit about uncertainty rather than bluffing.
- Respecting that people have different levels of experience with Kubernetes, Helm and .NET, and
  that "obvious" is relative.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **andmghan@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove
comments, close issues, or block accounts that do not follow this document.
